This is the first track of the book: **sixty days** that build one real
application. Fifty build days — one lesson each — and ten review days, one at
the end of every part. A build day takes about two to four hours; a review day
about one and a half. The days don't have to be consecutive: the plan is a
pace, not a deadline.

The app is complete and deployable by Day 60. Days 61–90 take it to
production, and days 91–100 cover data structures and algorithms.

Your progress is saved **in this browser only** — nothing about it leaves
your device.
